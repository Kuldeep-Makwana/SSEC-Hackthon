About

Here are some details of us like what we are and our vision,mission and goals.

Shantilal Shah Engineering College is approved by and affiliated to the Gujarat Technological University. It is accredited by All India Council for Technical Education, New Delhi and Government of Gujarat. The institute started functioning from the academic year 1983–84.

Shantilal Shah Engineering College is located in Bhavnagar, Gujarat. The span of programs offered 12 courses include Degree in Full Time mode. Shantilal Shah Engineering College offers a range of 7 UG courses. These courses are aimed to provide students with the necessary skills and knowledge in their field of interest. Candidate can pursue in multiple range of courses like M.E./M.Tech, B.E. / B.Tech with the stream of Engineering. From introductory courses to more advanced topics, there are a variety of courses to choose from. Students who join Shantilal Shah Engineering College stand to gain professional level of expertize and proficiency through its highly trained & experienced faculty in the studies of Civil Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Information Technology, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering. Courses offered by institute are well recognized and acknowlegded by approving body like AICTE. Institute offers this course with intake of 870 candidates. Most of these courses are offerred at very affordable fees.
